date: "2026-10-02"

summary: "I as a developer have equal power as a company to do whatever I want, and still have a life."
---

People complain about AI usage, but actually it is giving freedom to individuals and developers like me to build whatever they wished for. It is like everyone's opinion matters, again. I as a developer have equal power as a company to do whatever I want, and still have a life.

AI does not replace sound reasoning and thought, and it should not. I still decide what Tasfer is, how it works, and what ships.

AI today is centralized: frontier models live on someone else's servers. That is the same problem Tasfer exists to fight, and it should not be that way. I wish someone would build a local model as powerful as today's frontier models, one that runs on your own machine.

I use AI to build Tasfer: to write code, to word the translations, and to design parts of the interface. I review what it makes.

Tasfer itself has no AI features. Your notes are never sent to an AI model, by us or by the app.
