# 2026-09-20

- Pause syncing with one of your devices, or with someone you share a space with; the choice is private to you, follows you to your other devices, and is never told to them
- The Archive is now the Timeline: alongside archived pages and spaces it lists what changed in each space — created, renamed, made personal, and who joined — with who did it and when
- Tables copy and paste like a spreadsheet; a selection covers the rectangle between its two ends, copies keep their formatting, and pasting a grid from a spreadsheet grows the table to fit
- Enter and Shift+Enter do the same thing in every block; Enter turns an empty heading, list item, quote or equation into a paragraph, and Shift+Enter leaves a code block, equation or table
- A code block shows its language as a caption above its first line.
- The spelling shortcut opens the word you just typed before jumping ahead, and spell check can now be switched on and off from the action center
- Onboarding is shorter: it creates your first space and nothing else — joining a space moved to Add space
- A collaborator's caret shows inside an inline formula, where it used to disappear
- A modified delete at the edge of a line joins the neighbouring block instead of doing nothing
- Links open in the installed app, where they could look like dead links before
- There is no "active space" any more: anything tied to a page uses that page's own space, and the move picker lists every page in the space
- The installed app's title bar and splash screen follow dark mode
- A script typed in an empty script box pairs with the same base, and the caret keeps clear of an integral sign's slanted overhang
- Cutting an equation no longer leaves a blank chip behind
- Bug fixes and other UI changes
